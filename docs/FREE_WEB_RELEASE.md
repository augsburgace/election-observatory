## What is available

All 50 states have research destinations, source coverage and historical reference material. Current participation is shown where compatible validated observations exist. Published investigations retain their exact state, election, metric, voting method, cutoff, chronology, baseline and revision.

The release includes current participation policies and supported historical EV comparisons; historical President, Senate, House and governor results; geographic research; polling policies; experimental Senate calls and their reasoning; historical feature tests; evidence inspectors; and 50 state report packets. Reports preserve their source appendices and analytical scope. Browser controls support published-section selection, reordering, captions and printable reports. Private notes and saved browser investigations remain in that browser's local storage.

The full verified contest catalog remains available for discovery. Local, judicial and legislative analytical scopes that have not been published are identified as unavailable. The web edition publishes declared query scopes generated by the same services as the local application. It does not host the full mutable FastAPI service or accept arbitrary remote analytical jobs. An unpublished filter, cutoff or revision requires a matching future publication or the full local edition.

## Evidence and forecast limits

Historical EV gaps remain visible. This release does not establish complete daily, same-stage EV history for every state, method, geographic level or prior election. Reported history and retrospective reconstruction retain separate chronology. Comparisons remain unavailable when dates, definitions, voting opportunities or geographic coverage cannot be reconciled.

Installed precinct maps show **historical candidate-return context**. They do not establish current residential-precinct early-voting coverage or reveal how current early voters voted. Registration, party history, demographic characteristics and geographic context do not identify candidate choices; unsupported categories and joint demographic filters remain unavailable.

Senate EV calls are **experimental conditional party-margin estimates**. Their historical evaluation has not established dependable predictive improvement, calibrated winner probabilities or statistical confidence intervals. Displayed sensitivity ranges describe changed assumptions. The reasoning explains the measured evidence, historical reference and assumptions behind each supported call.

Polling, market and analyst-context switches default off. Optional context can explain supporting or conflicting evidence while preserving the EV estimate. Market prices are not vote shares. Public-browser RCP captures retain their actual capture dates and do not refresh unattended; original producer sources have their own collection paths and dates.

Only public aggregate outputs, public geometry and source citations are hosted. SQLite databases, original voter records, raw source archives, private research objects, credentials and local filesystem paths are excluded. Source attribution, including UF Election Lab/Michael P. McDonald tracker credits and transformation caveats, is retained in the public evidence.

## Automatic updates and continuity

The existing launchd job, `local.battleground-senate-observatory.collect`, is installed to run `scripts/update_web.py` every **7,200 seconds**. `RunAtLoad` is **false**. The first release was published and verified manually; the next scheduled interval had **not yet executed** when this release record was created. Installation alone is not evidence of a successful scheduled publication.

The configured updater was also executed manually with `--skip-collect`. It returned `unchanged` at revision 588 and retained the verified production deployment. The launchd environment resolves both Node and pnpm. Its installation proof and this no-change check are retained locally; they do not claim a later scheduled collection has already run.

The Mac must be awake, logged in and online for collection and publication. The schedule does not wake it. The hosted site remains available with the last successful release while the Mac is asleep or offline. Individual source failures and source publication schedules can also delay fresher evidence; the website's publication date and source reporting dates must be read separately.

Each future run collects due sources and, when evidence is newer, takes a consistent local checkpoint, builds and verifies the sanitized aggregate publication, compiles the application, checks excluded runtime files and hosting limits, and verifies the hosted reader after deployment. The complete manual privacy audit recorded above is separate from those automated publication gates. Pre-deployment failure retains the existing release. Failed post-deployment verification attempts a verified rollback to the prior successful production deployment. Equal or older evidence revisions do not overwrite a newer hosted investigation.
