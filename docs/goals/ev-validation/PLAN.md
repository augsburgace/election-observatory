# Historical EV and Senate validation — execution contract

Goal: finish the feasible historical early-vote collection and empirical Senate evaluation paths, compare equivalent documented stages across cycles, and deepen the forecasting workspace with linked maps, reasoning, sensitivity and honest confidence.

Intent: battleground Senate research first, polls and betting excluded from the initial EV projection. Truth owner: archived original authority evidence, explicitly attributed original-producer research bridges, reviewed definitions and native/canonical geographies, actual final Senate outcomes. Contract: archive -> validated dated aggregates/calendars -> stage features -> prior-only training/evaluation -> same service in maps, explanations, reports and exports. Cutover: local app only; current v1/v2 URLs and revision pins remain compatible. Displaced behavior: the zero-EV-evaluation claim is replaced only when real held-out cases have been computed. Existing probability gate remains separate.

Parallel task ownership is explicit. Only one coordinated collector process mutates live evidence. Pre-change backup must match the live revision (currently 242, advanced by the existing scheduled collector since the prior release). No source archives or private notebooks are overwritten. Data remains outside Git.

Acceptance: real historical Senate cases at supported 30/14/7/1-day or documented opening-stage cutoffs; no later label or final-turnout denominator enters its features; rolling-origin training excludes future cycles and calibration does not see held-out errors. Multiple snapshots of one race are never advertised as independent elections. Contemporary reporting and later reconstructed/datelabeled reports receive separate strata. Models that fail held-out improvement or lack independent evidence remain experimental. Probability/coverage/accuracy language must correspond to the actual evaluation.

Kill criteria / forbidden moves: invented observations, interpolated source facts, guessed opening calendars, unverified same-stage claims, early party registration treated as candidate votes, demographic/religious individual inference, outcome leakage, tuning on test elections, cherry-picked validations, hiding unavailable states, bypassing access controls, paid services or public deployment.

Final gates: focused and integrated tests, source/report arithmetic, actual acquisition and persisted case-ledger/model manifests, source-backed visual QA, pinned-view/reproducible-report compatibility, local backup/recovery coverage and dated limitations. Exact all-state source completeness is not inferred from a polished screen.
